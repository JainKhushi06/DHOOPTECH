# SolarSmart-NER

PS ID : 26005
PS TITLE : Solar Powered Smart Mini Cold Storage System for Fresh Vegetables in North Eastern Region (NER)

Our project addresses the problem of post-harvest losses of fresh vegetables and horticultural produce in the North Eastern Region (NER), where farmers often face limited access to affordable cold-storage facilities, unreliable electricity, difficult terrain, and transportation delays. The proposed solution is a Solar-Powered Smart Mini Cold Storage System that provides decentralized cooling closer to farms, collection centres, cooperatives, and local markets.

The system combines renewable energy, thermoelectric cooling, insulation, and smart monitoring to create a compact and scalable cold-storage solution.

☀️ Solar-Powered Operation: Solar panels act as the primary energy source and charge a rechargeable battery, enabling the system to operate even when direct sunlight is unavailable.
❄️ Peltier-Based Cooling: Peltier thermoelectric modules are used to cool the storage chamber. When DC current passes through the module, one side becomes cold and absorbs heat from inside the chamber, while the other side becomes hot.
🌡️ Heat Dissipation: The heat generated on the hot side is transferred to aluminium heatsinks and dissipated using DC cooling fans, which is essential for maintaining effective cooling.
🧊 Insulated Storage Chamber: Proper thermal insulation helps minimize heat entering the chamber and improves the overall cooling performance.
📡 Smart Monitoring: Temperature and humidity sensors connected to an ESP32 continuously monitor the internal storage conditions. The system can further be integrated with an IoT interface for real-time monitoring and alerts.
🔋 Battery Backup: The battery stores solar energy and provides a stable power supply to the cooling and monitoring system, allowing operation during periods of low or no sunlight.
🛡️ Protected Electronics: The battery, controller, and other electrical components can be placed in a protected enclosure to improve safety and suitability for outdoor/rural deployment.
📦 Modular & Scalable Design: The system can be adapted according to storage requirements by increasing the cooling capacity, insulation, and storage space.

The prototype consists of an insulated storage chamber, Peltier modules, aluminium heatsinks, cooling fans, solar panel, rechargeable battery, power-management circuitry, temperature and humidity sensors, and an ESP32-based monitoring system. A reviewer can operate the prototype by powering the system through the solar-battery setup and observing the cooling of the insulated chamber while monitoring temperature and humidity through the connected monitoring interface.

The expected impact of the project is to provide an affordable, renewable-energy-based and decentralized cold-storage option for small farmers and rural collection centres. By bringing storage facilities closer to the point of harvest, the system aims to extend the shelf life of fresh produce, reduce post-harvest losses, minimize dependence on unreliable grid electricity, and improve the efficiency of the local agricultural supply chain.
